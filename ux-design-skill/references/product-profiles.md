# Product profiles

Read the profile for the product you are designing before you apply the core rules. Precedence: the user's explicit instructions, then the product's existing system, then `SKILL.md` defaults.

## Quixera

- Personas in order: the pitch-side coach on a phone, the non-technical admin, and the parent. Design for the most frequent user first.
- Coach screens are mobile first and one-thumb, with primary actions in the bottom third and drills before theory.
- Brand primary is #0D7C66 with DM Sans. Never use the old #1D6B45.
- Plain language follows the product glossary. Coach content uses coaching points; family content uses one "I can" sentence.
- Sample data proves the multi-sport case, not cricket only.
- Design work lives in a demo replica only. The live app is read-only.

## KRCA

- Brand is KKR purple #1c0837 and gold #e6c22d.
- Audience: US South Asian diaspora parents and expats.
- Visuals are photoreal fictional youth players in US settings.
- Never use photos of real identifiable children in public posts.
- Primary call to action: a free trial class.

## TradeDesk

- Research and education only, never financial advice.
- The reader has no finance background: every term gets a plain-English definition and the verdict comes first.
- Baseline is `mock-b.md`. The editorial-ledger style may vary per brief; the three-role type, pill system, collapsed-by-default cards and breakpoint-gated layout stay.

## WagWise

- Household dog-care app. Mobile first, with large touch targets.
- Persona: the household member doing daily care; design for quick, glanceable action.
- Guidance is compact action items with countdown-style windows, based on actual and historical data.
- Trends come from entered data only. Never show a trend the user did not enter.
- Brand tokens: not yet defined. Ask the user for a brand direction, or propose 2–3 styles per the style rule in `SKILL.md`. Do not invent a brand color.

## Adding a profile

Each new profile needs: personas in frequency order, brand tokens (or a stated lack of them), type choices, the primary call to action, and any content rules such as photo policy or claim limits.
