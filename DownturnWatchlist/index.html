How the list was built

Reproducible screen + expert debate

Universe: 1,147 tickers in TradeDesk fundamentals. Original screen: market cap ≥ $2B; debt/equity ≤ 0.50; ROE ≥ 15%; profit margin ≥ 15%; revenue YoY ≥ +5%; revenue 3-year CAGR ≥ +5%; trailing P/E 0–40. 57 passed. The Nov 20 expert debate (value vs growth vs current-downturn tracks — Lee, Ives, Kelly, Goldman, Saxo, Wood vs Grantham, Gensler, Klement, Burry, Sløk, Dalio; Damodaran, Marks, Druckenmiller, Buffett in the middle) then added ten tests: (1) FCF / owner-earnings yield — FCF must be positive; (2) refinancing risk — net debt/FCF ≤ 5×, debt/EBITDA ≤ 3×; (3) pricing power — gross margin level, under 25% is thin; (4) AI-revenue quality — DSO trend, guarantees, customer concentration (qualitative this pass); (5) capex ROI — capex intensity + FCF margin, flag when capex > 25% of revenue AND FCF margin < 15%; (6) customer concentration; (7) credit regime gate; (8) breadth confirmation (S&P vs 200-day); (9) rotation / applications bucket; (10) event calendar.

What changed in v1.1.0: GOOGL (FCF −$1.2B, capex 38.4%) and AMZN (FCF −$34.7B, net debt $190.6B) moved to Builders Watch; WPM dropped (gold is a level-based trade; NEM stays). New Tier 5 (Rotation & applications): AAPL — fails the old screen on gross D/E only (0.84), overturned by the net-debt lens (ND/FCF 0.4×) — plus QLYS, ALL, CINF. The 26 break down 7 + 2 + 8 + 5 + 4.

Debt/equity is shown as a ratio. Zero-debt large caps effectively don’t exist — the lowest in the universe is 0.001, the median 0.69. This is the lowest-debt quality cohort: D/E 0.01–0.50 (AAPL 0.84 is the Tier 5 exception, carried by net debt, not gross debt). QoQ is the last two quarter-over-quarter revenue changes, most recent last; it is noisy for seasonal names (Intuit tax season, Deckers footwear). FCF = operating cash flow − capex, TTM, from Yahoo financialData.

Data quality — read this before trusting a cell: Yahoo’s annual/quarterly statement modules are degraded on the free endpoint (gross profit and EBIT return 0, balance sheets return empty), so margin trend and DSO trend are NOT computable — recorded as gaps, not faked. Foreign ADRs report in local currency (TSM TWD, PDD/TCOM CNY, SKHY KRW): FCF dollars and yields are artefacts (TSM’s true yield ≈1.8%, PDD’s ≈6.3%); ratios are currency-neutral and valid. SKHY and TCOM have no FCF in Yahoo at all (shown n/a, not zero). Insurers’ FCF is a rough proxy (capex near-meaningless for them); HIG’s 20.2% FCF yield is not trusted — HIG was NOT added. WPM’s FCF (−$3.1B, capex 172% of revenue) is anomalous and was not used as a drop reason on its own.

NOT added as screens, and why: insider buying — explicitly rejected (none of the five value thinkers uses it); buybacks / shareholder yield — contested, dangerous if debt-funded; relative strength / institutional flows as a quality screen — contested, and 13F data is 45–75 days stale (timing overlay only); own-history multiples as a hard screen — a 5-year history IS the bubble (10-year context only).

Honourable mentions — passed the screen but held up well (−4% to −15% off highs), so they are quality names, not bounce names: GRMN, SPOT, CBOE, CME, HIG, COCO, ABNB. (QLYS, ALL and CINF moved into Tier 5 in v1.1.0.)

Invalidation: if the AI-capex story breaks (hyperscaler capex cuts, memory price rollover), Tier 1’s growth case breaks with it. Re-screen weekly while the downturn lasts, and after each event on the calendar above.
