How the list was built

Reproducible screen

Universe: 1,147 tickers in TradeDesk fundamentals. Screen: market cap ≥ $2B; debt/equity ≤ 0.50; ROE ≥ 15%; profit margin ≥ 15%; revenue YoY ≥ +5%; revenue 3-year CAGR ≥ +5%; trailing P/E 0–40. 57 passed; the 25 below were ranked by quality plus how far they have fallen, with sector caps and judgment calls (GOOG deduped; NHI dropped — a 152% margin is a data error; foreign OTC ADRs dropped; weak-quarter names dropped).

Debt/equity is shown as a ratio. Zero-debt large caps effectively don’t exist — the lowest in the universe is 0.001, the median 0.69. This is the lowest-debt quality cohort: D/E 0.01–0.50. QoQ is the last two quarter-over-quarter revenue changes, most recent last; it is noisy for seasonal names (Intuit tax season, Deckers footwear).

Honourable mentions — passed the screen but held up well (−4% to −15% off highs), so they are quality names, not bounce names: GRMN, SPOT, QLYS, CBOE, CME, HIG, COCO, ABNB.

Invalidation: if the AI-capex story breaks (hyperscaler capex cuts, memory price rollover), Tier 1’s growth case breaks with it. Re-screen weekly while the downturn lasts.
