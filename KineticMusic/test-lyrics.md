different voices and melody shapes, to hear how tempo and phrasing change
the song.

## What to listen for

- **Syllable → note mapping**: every syllable gets its own sung pitch.
  Long words split into multiple notes ("satellites" → sa-tel-lites).
- **Tempo**: BPM slider changes syllable speed *and* the backing pluck.
- **Note length**: eighth notes = rapid-fire; half notes = ballad.
- **Voices**: all four are original formant synthesizers — no samples,
  no artist imitation, nothing copyrighted.
- **Sheet music**: every sung syllable is transcribed on the staff with
  its lyric written under the note. Hit **⤓ MIDI** to download the song
  and open it in any DAW; **⤴ Share link** copies a URL that replays it.
