What changed in this redesign — and why

  1. One attention queue, ranked. The 22-item error/warning list plus scattered badges become 5 plain-language items, ordered by money and urgency. Alert fatigue was the #1 complaint pattern.
  2. Plain words. "Money owed" not Outstanding / AR aging; "How full are my classes" not Utilization; "Failed payments" not Dunning.
  3. One Programs, one People. Programs and Programs v2 merge into a single builder; Athletes, Customers, Memberships and Inquiries stop being four overlapping "people" lists.
  4. Schedule defaults to simple. Day view with the sessions; lane/layout/density controls move behind "More filters" for the power users.
  5. Blocking facts surface. Missing waivers, overdue money and unmarked attendance appear on the home screen — never two tabs deep.
  6. Everything is still here. Simplicity is the default view, not removed capability — every screen stays one search away.
