My check: market sizes

Independent software-market reports put youth sports software at ~$506M globally in 2025 (→$1.64B by 2035, 12.5% CAGR) and sports league software at $1.25B (→$2.94B by 2032). Your $1.2B Academy figure is defensible only if it counts adjacent spend (payments volume, services) — pure software TAM is smaller. The honest TAM story is share of the $40B+ families spend plus payments flowing through your rails, not software licences alone.

What's right. The core thesis survives my research: monetize the household, price on aspiration not features, sequence expensive CV last, and the Workload Guardian is genuinely the best anti-churn mechanic in youth sports software — nobody else can build an ECB-standard safety feature. The per-fan billing insight (Fan Pass) is the smartest unit-of-billing call in the memo.
Where I'd push back.
1. TAM is overstated. Pure software TAM is ~$0.5B globally, not $1.2B US. The pitch that works is share of the $40B family spend + payments volume — don't let a diligence process catch the bigger number undefended.
2. The moat is rented. Everything in the $199 Pathway tier depends on what the LAKR agreement actually permits in a paid consumer SKU — brand use, badge issuance, selection linkage. Your memo flags this as Monday item #1 and it's still the #1 risk. If scope narrows, Pathway pricing collapses to "app with videos."
3. 35% attach is aggressive. It's defensible only if three things all fire: checkout placement + 25% rev-share + coach endorsement. NCSA converts with high-pressure sales; you're proposing to convert with product. The Phase-0 pilot is the whole ballgame — fund nothing in Phase 2 until it reports.
4. SportsEngine/NBC is underplayed in the memo — it's the direct threat to Pipeline (NGB relationships) and it's already bundling streaming.
5. Execution bandwidth. 615 stories, guardian app at zero, AI layer at zero — the roadmap is a lot for one team. The memo's "cheapest first" sequencing is the right defense; protect it against scope creep.
The one-line bet. If the workload guardian + weekly recap ship in the guardian app and attach clears 20% at winter registration, the model works. Everything else is optimization.
