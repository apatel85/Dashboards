Data & Methodology
🏛️ HUD Fair Market Rents — FY2027

ZIP-level Small Area FMRs published by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. Extracted 2026-09-28 from the official file:

FY27_safmrs.xlsx (4.4 MB)

Effective October 1, 2026. FMRs are gross rent estimates (shelter rent + utilities) set at the 40th percentile of standard-quality rental units.

🏘️ Census ACS — 2024 5-Year

Median home value, household income, tenure and vacancy from the American Community Survey 5-year estimates (2020–2024), fetched live in your browser from the Census Reporter API — no key required:

api.censusreporter.org

Tables: B25077 (home value) · B19013 (income) · B25003 (tenure) · B25002 (vacancy). Cached 7 days in your browser; a “CACHED” badge shows when you're viewing stored data.

🧮 Derived metrics
  • Gross yield = (FMR 2BR × 12) ÷ median home value
  • Price-to-rent = median home value ÷ (FMR 2BR × 12)
  • Affordability = (FMR 2BR × 12) ÷ median household income
  • Renter % = renter-occupied ÷ total occupied units
  • Vacancy % = vacant ÷ total housing units
🏆 Composite score (0–100)

Each metric is min-max normalized across the 29 markets, then weighted:

  • Gross yield — 40%
  • Renter demand — 20%
  • Low vacancy — 15%
  • Affordability (low rent burden) — 15%
  • Median income — 10%

Grades: A ≥ 75 · B ≥ 60 · C ≥ 45 · D < 45. ZIPs missing a Census estimate are scored on available metrics only, flagged with “—”.

⚠️ Honest limits
  • FMR is a benchmark, not a guarantee of achievable rent.
  • ACS 5-year data lags the market by 1–2 years by design.
  • STR figures are scenarios you set — no free source publishes verified STR revenue by ZIP.
  • This is research tooling, not financial advice.
🔌 Why no Supabase / API keys?

Every number here comes from free public sources that need no key and no backend. Adding accounts, logins and a database would add friction without making any number more real. If you later want watchlists, alerts or saved comparisons, that's when a Supabase invest_iq schema earns its place.
