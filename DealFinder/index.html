Why DealFinder

From the Phase 0 research, November 2026 — competitor facts from published reviews, with sources.

The gap

None of the three apps checked does the whole household job. Flipp browses flyers but never judges whether a price is a deal; ShopSavvy looks up one barcode at a time but never prices a whole list; Basket priced the whole list and died because its prices were crowdsourced. DealFinder's edge: the whole list, priced live from retailer data, with a per-unit verdict and a household list — no account, no personal information.

Flipp — every flyer, no verdict

Weekly flyers from 800+ retailers in one app, smart lists matched to deals, price matching, digital coupons. Free; 4.8★ from ~401,000–412,629 ratings; 10M+ installs (26M per AppBrain).

  • No price-history diary — no way to tell whether today's price is actually a deal.
  • No price-drop alerts; flyers expire weekly, so saved items revert to full price.
  • Smaller, ethnic, and specialty stores may not participate; price matching depends on the retailer's own policy, not the app.

Sources: mobexer.com · usdollarpantry.com · mylinovar.com · appbrain.com

Basket — priced the whole list, and it's gone

Built a list and showed totals by store — one 15-item test: $62 at Walmart, $71 at Kroger, $58 at Aldi — across 100+ chains, with prices usually within a dollar or two of checkout. It is no longer on the US App Store (checked Oct 30, 2026).

Its prices were crowdsourced from shoppers — only as fresh as the last shopper who reported — and coverage thinned the moment people stopped reporting. The lesson DealFinder is built on: never crowdsourced prices; prices come from retailer / flyer data checked at search time.

Sources: groceriestracker.com · groceryroutes.com

ShopSavvy — one barcode at a time

Barcode scan + multi-retailer price comparison. Free with in-app purchases; 4.0–4.2★ from ~110,000–114,000 ratings; 10–17M installs. Fast in-store scan, price history, price-drop alerts, price-match guidance at checkout.

  • No quality or value score — it gives the cheapest price, not whether the item is worth buying.
  • Weak on loose produce and anything without a barcode; no scan history; results skew online rather than local shelf prices.
  • Recent user reviews report black screens, ad overload, and scans returning nothing.

Sources: mobexer.com · withguava.io · appbrain.com · play.google.com

Words, in plain language

  • UPC — the barcode number on a package.
  • Unit price — price per ounce / per pound, so different sizes can be compared fairly.
  • Price match — a store selling it at a competitor's lower price.
  • Loyalty price — the lower price for members of the store's free card.
  • Flyer (circular) — the store's weekly deals paper.
  • Rebate / cashback — money back after you buy, instead of a lower price at the register.

How prices work here

Prices come from retailer and flyer data, checked at search time — never crowdsourced from other shoppers. Every price shows its store and when it was checked. No account, no sign-up, no personal information: your list lives in this browser only.

Design rules from the DealFinder Phase 0 Receipt, 2026-11-12.

Live now — what this version does

  • Real flyer prices for your ZIP, checked weekly when each store's new circular drops. Every price shows its store and when the deal ends.
  • 30-day history: one price point per item per check, kept 30 days; alert = today's best price below the 30-day average. Only one check exists so far, so history is still building.
  • ShopRite isn't here yet — it has no flyer on the current feed for this ZIP. A second source for it is planned.
  • Alerts stay in-app in this version — nothing is sent anywhere. Your list and tracked items live in this browser only.
