## 1. Developmental Stages & Timeline

| Stage | Age | What's happening |
|---|---|---|
| Neonatal | Birth–2 weeks | Blind, deaf; can't regulate temperature or toilet alone [1] |
| Transitional | 2–3 weeks | Eyes/ears open; standing, walking, tail-wagging; baby teeth begin [1] |
| Socialization | 3–12 weeks | Rapid brain growth (peaks ~7 wks); senses fully functional by 4 wks; learning capacity complete by 12 wks; fastest learning period — housetraining foundations laid here [1] |
| Fear-impact period | 8–11 weeks | Single bad experiences can imprint lasting fears; keep exposures short and positive [2] |
| Juvenile | 12 wks–sexual maturity | Motor skills refined; baby teeth lost 12–16 wks; full adult teeth by ~6 mo; bladder control ~20 wks [2] |
| Second fear period | 5–12 months | New fears can appear suddenly; don't force confrontations, build confidence [2] |
| Adolescence | ~6–18 months | Puberty; boundary-testing, possible training regression [3] |
| Maturity | 1–4 years | Social maturity; behavior patterns solidify [3] |

**Cavapoo-specific growth:** Toy Poodles are generally done growing by ~6–7 months; Cavapoos reach most of adult size by ~8–10 months and finish filling out by ~12 months (one source says basically done by 18 months) [4][5].

**Simba marker:** At ~18 weeks he is mid-**juvenile period** — peak teething, bladder control still maturing (~20 wks), past the first fear window, approaching the **second fear period (5–12 months)**.

## 2. Temperament & Trainability

**Consensus profile:** affectionate, gentle, playful, people-focused ("velcro dog"), intelligent, eager to please; good with children and other pets; medium energy [6][7].

**What works:**
- **Positive reinforcement is the universal recommendation** — treats, praise, play, short consistent sessions. Cavaliers are described as loving to please; Poodles as highly trainable [10].
- Peer-reviewed data (BSAVA Science Digest, 2024, summarizing PLOS ONE studies on designer Poodle-crosses vs. purebred parents): Cavapoos scored **better than either parent breed on trainability** — i.e., measurably more trainable than Cavaliers or Poodles [11].

**What doesn't work / backfires:**
- **Harsh correction.** Cavapoos are emotionally sensitive and attuned; yelling, harsh tones, or household tension cause anxiety and unwanted behaviors (barking, destructive chewing) [8][9].
- The same peer-reviewed comparison found Cavapoos showed **more** undesirable behaviors than parent breeds on 8 scales: owner/stranger/dog-directed aggression, stranger/dog-directed fear, non-social fear, **separation-related problems, and excitability** [11]. (Note: "more than progenitors" is relative — it doesn't mean Cavapoos are aggressive dogs in absolute terms.)

**Therapy-dog suitability:** the sweet, gentle temperament makes them good therapy-dog candidates [6].

**Source-reliability flag:** petlur.com claims the Cavapoo "is recognized as a breed by several major kennel clubs, including the American Kennel Club" — this is **false**; the AKC does not recognize designer crosses [6]. Treat that site's other claims with caution.

## 3. Exercise Needs by Age

- **Puppy structured walks:** ~5 minutes per month of age, split into short sessions (e.g., ~25 min/day at 5 months). Avoid high jumps, long stair sessions, and extended running in puppies [12].
- **Adults:** 30–60 minutes/day total (walks + play), split into two walks plus indoor activity and mental enrichment [10][13][14].
- **Caution:** don't over-exercise growing puppies — long-term joint impact [41].

**Disagreement:** adult totals vary (30 min vs. 30–60 vs. 45–60 min across sources) — consensus floor is ~30 min; the spread reflects sources, not real conflict. The 5-min/month puppy rule was found in only one Cavapoo-specific source here (it's widely repeated general advice) — see "Could not verify."

## 4. Coat Type & Grooming

**Three coat variations** (driven by genetics/generation — F1, F1b, F2):

| Coat | Look | Shedding | Grooming effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fleece | Soft, wavy, light | Low | Medium |
| Wool | Dense, curly, Poodle-like | Very low | High — daily brushing |
| Hair | Straighter, Cavalier-like | Medium–high | Low [15] |

- **Brushing:** 2–3×/week minimum; curly/wool coats need daily brushing. Matting-prone zones: behind ears, armpits, belly, collar area [15][17].
- **Professional grooming:** every 4–6 weeks [17][6] **vs.** every 6–8 weeks [16] — **disagreement**; consensus "every 4–8 weeks," with coat type and trim length explaining the spread.
- **Bathing:** every 4–6 weeks with gentle dog shampoo; brush before bathing; dry thoroughly (damp coat → skin issues) [17].
- **"Hypoallergenic" marketing is overstated:** no dog is 100% hypoallergenic — dander and saliva proteins trigger allergies, not just hair [6].

## 5. Health Predispositions (Both Parent Breeds)

### 5.1 Mitral Valve Disease (MVD) — primarily the Cavalier side
- The Cavalier's leading cause of death; prevalence ~20× other breeds; **lifetime prevalence approaches 100% in CKCS** (Prof. Melanie Hezzell, veterinary cardiology, June 2025) vs. ~14% in non-CKCS breeds [18].
- **Onset:** heart murmur typically detected from ~3–4 years (males) / 4–5 years (females); once diagnosed, Cavaliers can reach life-threatening stage in 1–3 years vs. 3–5 in other breeds [19].
- **Owner watch-list:** heart murmur (earliest sign, vet-detected); coughing (after exercise/at night); exercise intolerance; labored breathing; lethargy; fainting/collapse; weight loss [20].
- **Note:** Toy/Miniature Poodles are also listed among small breeds susceptible to MVD — both parent breeds contribute risk [18].
- **Framing disagreement:** some sources cite murmurs "as early as 1–2 years" (earliest reports) vs. the typical 3–6 year window — earliest-case vs. typical-case framing, not a contradiction.

### 5.2 Syringomyelia (SM) — the Cavalier side
- Inherited, progressive neurological disease: fluid-filled cavities (syrinxes) in the spinal cord caused by Chiari-like malformation (skull too small for the brain).
- **Onset:** typically **6 months–4 years**; leading researcher Dr. Clare Rusbridge (veterinary neurologist) reports symptoms appear between 6 months and 4 years in 85% of symptomatic dogs; PetMD notes diagnosis usually 6 months–3 years, reported up to 10 years [21].
- **Hallmark owner watch-list:** "phantom"/air scratching at the neck/shoulder (often one-sided, while walking, worse with collar/excitement); yelping for no reason; neck pain/sensitivity; head shaking; weakness or wobbly hind legs [21]. Researchers consider scratching a sign of pain/neurological damage.
- **Diagnosis:** MRI only. Comfort measures: harness instead of collar, elevated food/water bowls [21].
- **Prevalence figure "30–70% of Cavaliers have syrinxes"** could not be independently cross-verified — see "Could not verify."

### 5.3 Patellar Luxation — both parent breeds (esp. Poodle side)
- Peer-reviewed (PLOS ONE, 2024, "doodle dilemma" study): **Cavapoos showed NO reduced patellar-luxation risk vs. either parent breed** — no hybrid-vigor benefit for this trait; small size plus a high-prevalence progenitor breed keeps risk up [22].
- **Watch-list:** intermittent skipping/hopping gait; sudden hind-leg lameness that resolves on its own. Can be noticeable at the puppy vet check [23]. Avoid high-impact jumping on/off furniture [24].

### 5.4 Hip Dysplasia — both breeds (esp. Poodle side)
- Recognized predisposition in Poodles; reputable Miniature Poodle breeders X-ray hips (OFA) before breeding [39]. The UC Davis/Hart poodle study tracks hip dysplasia among joint disorders in the breed [40].
- **Related small-breed watch:** **Legg-Calvé-Perthes disease** — hip-joint disease of young small-breed dogs, onset **4–12 months**; watch for hind-leg lameness/limping in that window [23]. **Simba marker (Oct 3):** he has just entered the 4–12 month watch window — flag any hind-leg lameness/limping to the vet promptly.
- Management: weight control, joint-supportive diet, vet monitoring [24].

### 5.5 Eye Conditions — both breeds
- **Cataracts:** can be congenital, juvenile, or age-related; PRA-associated secondary cataracts noted in Poodles [25].
- **PRA (progressive retinal atrophy):** both CKCS and Poodle varieties are listed among commonly affected breeds; starts with night blindness, progresses to full vision loss; DNA tests exist for some forms [26].
- **Retinal dysplasia/folds:** present from birth; best examined by a vet ophthalmologist at **12–16 weeks** when the retina is mature [23]. **Simba marker (Oct 3):** at ~18 weeks the 12–16 week exam window has closed — if a vet ophthalmologist exam wasn't done, ask the regular vet whether a dilated eye exam at the next visit would still be worthwhile.
- **Owner watch-list:** bumping into objects, cloudy/blue eye, night-vision difficulty, dilated pupils, redness/discharge.

### 5.6 Ear Infections — both breeds
- Floppy ears trap moisture/debris → bacterial/yeast growth; Poodles' hairy ear canals add wax buildup [27].
- **Prevention:** keep ears clean and dry (dry after baths/swims); regular gentle cleaning with vet-approved cleaner; trim inner-ear hair for airflow; check 2–3×/week for odor, redness, head-shaking, scratching [17][28].

### 5.7 Recommended Health Screenings (ask the breeder / verify)
- **Minimum for both parents:** board-certified **cardiologist heart clearance + board-certified ophthalmologist eye clearance, dated within the year**; patellar luxation check annually; OFA hip X-rays at 2+ years; MRI is the only definitive SM test [29].
- Reputable Cavapoo breeders: OFA heart, patella, eyes, hips/elbows + DNA panel (e.g., Embark-style, 270+ conditions) [30][31].

## 6. Adult Size & Lifespan

**Cavapoo adult size** (depends on Toy vs. Miniature Poodle parent — **verify with breeder**):
- Overall: ~9–14 inches tall, **~10–25 lbs** [14]; another source lists 9–20 lbs [6] — **disagreement reflects Toy-vs-Mini parent variation**.
- Toy Cavapoo (Toy Poodle parent): ~7–13 lbs, ~12 in tall [4][5].
- Mini Cavapoo (Miniature Poodle parent): ~13–25 lbs, 12–16 in [4][5].
- Parent reference: Toy Poodle ≤10 in / 4–6 lbs; Miniature Poodle 10–15 in / 10–15 lbs [32].

**Simba marker:** 7.5 lbs at ~17 weeks tracks toward the middle of the range; final size hinges on the Poodle parent's variety.

**Lifespan:** Cavapoo **10–15 years** (two independent sources agree) [6][34]. Parents: Toy Poodle median 14y 8m / Miniature 13y 11m (2004 UK breed survey) [32]; Cavalier 9–14 years (majority) [33] vs. one outlier source claiming 10–16. **No large Cavapoo-specific longevity survey exists** — the 10–15 figure is extrapolated from parent-breed data.

## 7. Puppy-Specific Watchlist

### Teething timeline
| Age | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 2–4 wks | Baby teeth erupt |
| 5–8 wks | Full set of 28 deciduous teeth |
| 12–16 wks | Baby teeth start falling out (incisors first) |
| 4–6 mo | Adult teeth erupt (canines ~4–5 mo, premolars/molars ~5–7 mo) |
| 6–7 mo | Full 42 adult teeth [35][36] |

- **Small-breed caution:** retained baby teeth are "particularly common in small and toy-breed dogs" — if baby teeth (esp. canines) remain at ~6 months alongside adult teeth, have the vet check; retained teeth cause misalignment and decay [37].
- **Simba marker:** at ~18 weeks he is in peak tooth-loss/chewing phase — expect chewing, drooling, mild gum bleeding, possible appetite dips.

### Socialization windows
- **Primary critical window: 3–12 weeks** [1]; commonly extended in practice to ~16 weeks.
- **Fear-impact period: 8–11 weeks** — single bad experiences can imprint lasting fears [2].
- **Second fear period: 5–12 months** — new fears may emerge; build confidence, don't force confrontations [2].
- **Simba marker:** just past the primary window — continued positive exposure still matters; second fear period approaching.

### Separation anxiety — elevated in this mix
- **Tendency confirmed:** peer-reviewed BSAVA/PLOS data shows Cavapoos have significantly more separation-related problems than either parent breed; breed guides list them as prone to it [11][9].
- **Prevention (expert consensus):** gradual desensitization to alone time from day one (seconds/minutes, building up); calm low-key departures/arrivals; reward calm independent behavior; safe comfortable space (crate/pen); mental enrichment (Kongs, lick mats, puzzle toys); adequate exercise before absences [9][38].

## 8. Where Experts Disagree (Summary)
1. **Adult exercise:** 30 min vs. 30–60 vs. 45–60 min/day — consensus floor ~30 min; spread reflects sources.
2. **Professional grooming interval:** 4–6 vs. 6–8 weeks — coat type and trim length explain it.
3. **Adult weight range:** 9–20 vs. 10–25 lbs — Toy-vs-Mini parent variation.
4. **Cavalier lifespan:** 9–14 (majority) vs. 10–16 (one outlier).
5. **MVD onset:** "as early as 1–2 years" (earliest reports) vs. typical 3–6 years — framing difference.

- SM prevalence figure "30–70% of Cavaliers have syrinxes" — single source, not independently cross-verified.
- Cavapoo-specific lifespan — no large longevity survey; 10–15 years is extrapolated.
