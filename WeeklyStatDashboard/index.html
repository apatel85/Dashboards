How the numbers are built — and backtested

Expected yards. Each player's per-game rate is first regressed toward his position's average (blended = (games × rate + 2 × position avg) ÷ (games + 2)), then scaled by this week's matchup with the multiplier shrunk halfway back to neutral (mult′ = 1 + 0.5 × (opp allowed ÷ league avg − 1)). League baselines through Week 6: 110.0 rush yards and 220.5 pass yards allowed per game.

Comfortable max is that expected number itself — his average outcome in this game against this defense, rounded to whole yards. It is not a season ceiling or a best-case tail. P(60+) uses a normal approximation with σ = 0.65 × expected yards; "likely" means P ≥ 55%.

Touchdowns count physical scorers only — a QB's passing TDs never count; a QB who runs it in does, on rushing TDs alone — none clears the bar this week, with Josh Allen on bye. TD rates get stronger regression (weight 4) than yardage rates, and a player needs at least 3 games of data to make the 1+ TD board. P(1+) = 1 − e^(−λ), P(2+) = 1 − e^(−λ)(1 + λ); "likely 1+ TD" means P ≥ 50%. Every row shows its games sample — thin samples are the main reason the raw model used to overpromise.

Honest limits. The TD board was the weakest output in the backtest (its "likely" flags hit 36% against 74% predicted before calibration) — that's why it gets the strictest treatment here, and why the 2+ TD board is a ranked watch list, not a promise: multi-TD games are rare and nobody is a lock. Five weeks of rolling backtest data is still a small sample, and the live model uses fuller game windows than the backtest could, so real-world calibration should run a bit better than the backtest showed.

Rolling backtest — Weeks 2–6

The calibration behind the Week 7 boards is no longer a fixed Weeks 2–5 snapshot. The backtest now rolls: every Wednesday it re-runs from Week 2 through the last completed week — strict no-lookahead, each week predicted only from data available before it — re-derives whether the current settings still hold, and only then are the new boards built. The window grows by one week each refresh: next Wednesday (Oct 28) it covers Weeks 2–7 for the Week 8 projections.
