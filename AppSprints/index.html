App Sprints

Tell us what you need built, in plain words. We turn it into a short research sprint first, so the design starts from evidence, not guesses.

Here is how it works. You fill in this form. That starts a Phase 0 research sprint: we check what already exists, who it is for, and what could go wrong. You get a Research Receipt back — a short, plain summary of what we found. Only then does design start.

How the pipeline works, in detail
  1. Intake (this form). Answer the questions below. It takes about five minutes.
  2. Phase 0 research sprint. We research the idea: similar tools, the people who will use it, and the risks. No building yet.
  3. Research Receipt. You get a short written summary of the findings, with a clear go or no-go.
  4. Design. If the receipt says go, design starts from the research, not from a blank page.

New intakes are picked up daily at about 8am ET. If you need one sooner, paste your brief in the App Sprints side chat and ask for a manual run.
