CricMax Connect

The working tools behind a youth sports academy platform (Quixera): a shared task tracker for the team, a dashboard that tracks UAT testing carried out by AI agents, and a browser app that lets real users flag an issue with a screenshot or video — saved straight into that dashboard.

RoleUAT testing, design & usability guidance, path-to-market advice
  • Supabase auth & sync
  • Works offline
  • UAT dashboard
  • Browser capture app
  • Marketing storyboard & automation
Live demo on request
Problem
An academy SaaS team coordinating work across spreadsheets and chat, testing scattered across people and agents, bugs reported as vague messages nobody could reproduce — and marketing planned ad hoc, post by post.
Built
CricMax Connect: a synced task tracker (Google + email sign-in, offline-capable). A UAT dashboard that tracks testing run by AI agents — what was tested, what passed or failed, and the evidence captured for each step. For actual users, a QA Capture app that integrates into the browser: flag an issue in the moment with a screenshot or screen recording plus a short description, and it is saved into the dashboard for the team to work from. And a marketing storyboard with automation for planning posts, scheduling them, and tracking what went out.
Result
My role is the operator’s one: hands-on UAT, design and usability direction, and guidance on the path to bring the product to market — plus a marketing approach drawn from running Edible’s campaigns, with the storyboard and automation doing the repetitive posting and tracking work.

TradeDesk

A market workstation I built for my own personal finances and investing — it pulls a live broker feed, earnings, news, and congressional activity into one daily brief, so I can spot opportunities against my investment goals and read it in minutes.

Scale~518 S&P 500 / Nasdaq-100 tickers, live Schwab quotes & options chains
  • Schwab API
  • Supabase
  • GitHub Actions pipelines
  • Backtest harness
Live demo on request
Problem
For my own investing, market information was scattered across a broker terminal, news feeds, and filings — hard to pull together into a view of where the opportunities were, with scoring ideas everywhere and no discipline about which ones actually work.
Built
A tabbed workstation — Daily Brief, Options, Signals, Earnings, Congress & Gov, Research, Deep Dive, Performance — fed by a read-only Schwab proxy (equities, ETFs, stock and index options, Greeks included), refreshed on a schedule, invite-only for a small group of users.
Result
Every scoring module runs through a backtest harness before it is trusted, and ideas that don’t hold up out-of-sample don’t make the screen. That restraint is the feature.
